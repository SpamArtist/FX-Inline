FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account to use FX Inline?

No. You can use FX Inline without creating an account.

Where are my settings stored?

Your preferences are stored locally in your browser profile.

Can I use this website without the extension installed?

Yes, this website is public. The conversion functionality itself requires installing the extension.
