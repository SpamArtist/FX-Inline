FX Inline helps you understand prices online at a glance. When you visit a webpage, it can show prices in your preferred currency directly beside the original amount, making it easier to compare products, subscriptions, travel costs, and international prices without opening a separate converter. You can choose your currency, turn automatic conversion on or off, and control whether conversion runs everywhere or only on the current page.

FX Inline is designed to stay out of the way: it only works when it finds price information and keeps the original price visible so you can always compare both amounts.
