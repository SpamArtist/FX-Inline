- Confusing settings make otherwise useful extensions feel unusable.
- CPU/memory overload on complex sites is a category risk.
- Intrusive rating prompts are a visible trust problem.
- Currency coverage gaps create recurring requests.

Revenue-relevant interpretation:

Consumer users like the category but expect it to be free. Merchants pay when localization is tied to sales, checkout confidence, setup and support. So the immediate revenue move should not be “add a Pro extension plan.” It should be “sell a business outcome with implementation help.”

- It is more defensible than generic consumer currency conversion.

Why not full SaaS backend now:

A hosted control plane is eventually useful, but building it before a paying pilot risks weeks of infrastructure work with no buyer proof.
